If this sounds familiar

You set the goals with energy in January. By March they're a distant memory.

Most strategic plans fail the same way. You spend an energized day writing down goals, then head back to the daily grind. There's no written plan with real context, no one else bought in, and nothing holding anyone accountable. The list ends up in a drawer somewhere.

A strategy is only worth something if it changes what happens on a Tuesday. The plan is the easy part. The hard part, the part most owners never get help with, is turning it into how the business actually runs.

More about me

Common questions

Business strategy, answered plainly.

What does a business strategy consultant actually do?

I help you set a clear direction for the business and, just as importantly, build the structure to carry it out. A plan is only worth anything if it changes what happens week to week, so I stay involved through the execution, not just the planning.

Do you just write a plan and leave?

No. The plan is the easy part. I work with you and your team to turn it into priorities, a meeting rhythm, and accountability, and I stay hands-on until your team owns it.

Is this a rigid system or framework?

No. The approach is built around your business and where it actually stands, not a one-size-fits-all program with my name on it. I bring what works and make it fit you.

Do you work remotely or in person?

Both. Planning work is often best in person around Langley, the Fraser Valley, and Metro Vancouver, with ongoing sessions over Microsoft Teams, and I work remotely across British Columbia.
